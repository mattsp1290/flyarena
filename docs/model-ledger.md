# Model ledger vocabulary

FlyArena separates source evidence from engineering choices. These labels must remain visible and unambiguous in the product and its documentation.

| Surface | Required label | Meaning |
| --- | --- | --- |
| Graph topology | **Measured** | Connections and contact counts come from the pinned source dataset. |
| Biological annotations | **Annotated** | Cell classes, transmitter assignments, and similar metadata are source annotations rather than direct measurements of simulated behavior. |
| Network dynamics | **Authored / literature-derived** | The rate equations and update rules are engineering choices informed by literature; they are not measured dynamics from the source animal. |
| Global parameters | **Calibrated** | Global gains or thresholds may be adjusted under a documented, shared calibration procedure. |
| Sensory encoder and action decoder | **Authored** | Input/output mappings are designed for this POC. The sensory encoder and `decodeAction` must remain identical across experimental arms in both decoder modes. In **Authored mode** (the default) the full decode path, including the population-sum aggregation, is also identical across arms. In **Trained mode** the decode path reads from a per-arm trained readout (see "Trained readout" below): its weights differ per arm by design (each arm is trained independently), but its architecture, trainable-parameter count, and training procedure are identical across arms. |
| Trained readout | **Trained (offline)** | A small `D → H → 3` MLP that replaces the population-sum aggregation when Trained mode is selected, reading the per-neuron rates of every output-assigned neuron. Weights are optimized offline by the cross-entropy method (CEM) against the arena score and shipped as a pinned, hashed static artifact (`public/data/trained-readout-v1.json`) — an engineering artifact, not biology, with identical architecture and trainable-parameter count across arms (gated at export time). See `docs/trained-readout-report.md` for the held-out evaluation; its headline per-arm numbers are measured single-agent with the opponent parked, which differs from the shipped two-agent side-by-side default — the report's side-by-side table covers that condition. |
| 3D presentation | **Synthetic** | Arena geometry, agents, effects, and camera are visual presentation, not biological anatomy or biomechanics. |
| Neuron positions | **Measured** | The anatomical activity view places each neuron at its MaleCNS soma annotation (or soma-tract fallback); a neuron with neither is shown in a labeled "position unavailable" strip, never at an invented coordinate. Units are the dataset's own voxel units and are explicitly disclosed as unverified (see `docs/data-provenance.md`). |
| Displayed neural activity | **Computed** | The activity view's per-tick colors are this POC's authored dynamics running on the measured topology, not a measurement of biological activity. |
| Topology null distribution | **Computed (offline)** | Descriptive scores of 500 degree-preserving rewirings of the biological graph (plus biological and disconnected reference points), all scored by the same authored decoder with the opponent parked, offline and ahead of time. It is not a biological measurement, and it makes no causal or superiority claim — see `docs/rewiring-null-report.md` for the full method, limitations, and results. `src/lib/ui/NullHistogram.svelte` renders the 500-rewiring histogram in the ledger panel's "Topology null distribution" section, with biological/rewired-seed-0/disconnected drawn as dash-patterned (not color-only) markers and a caption stating biological's empirical percentile. If the pinned `rewiring-null-v1.json` artifact is missing, the section is simply absent; if it fails its sha256 check or shape validation, the section instead shows an honest "failed verification" message — neither state affects the rest of the experiment. |
| Null-result explanation | **Computed (offline)** | A descriptive analysis, under this model only, of why biological scored where it did in the topology null distribution above: whether a decoder sign convention, a linear input→output signal pathway, or a structural graph feature is associated with the low score, each evaluated against predeclared thresholds and reported alongside a permutation-based chance rate. It is not a biological claim, and a structural-feature finding may be definition-sensitive (see `docs/null-explanation-report.md`'s "Structural features" section for the feature-6/`weightedInDegree` disclosure). `src/lib/ui/LedgerPanel.svelte` renders the finding sentence, every qualifying metric in plain words (flagging the definition-sensitive one), the mirrored-decoder-check result, and the regime-check outcome directly under the null histogram, with a link to `docs/null-explanation-report.md`. If the pinned `null-explanation-v1.json` artifact is missing (no manifest entry at all), the note is simply absent with no message; if it could not be fetched (a network failure), it shows an honest "could not be loaded" message; if it fails its sha256 check, shape validation, or its cross-check against the shipped rewiring-null artifact, it instead shows an honest "failed verification" message — none of these states affects the rest of the experiment or the histogram above it. |
| Pathway intervention test | **Computed (offline)** | A predeclared, degree-preserving swap-set intervention along the clearance→thrust pathway the null-result explanation above flagged, scored against matched degree-preserving controls (unrestricted and class-matched) under both the authored decoder and CEM-retrained readouts, mechanically evaluated against predeclared outcome categories. It is not a biological claim, a positive result means the net effect of the accepted swap set (not a single-edge causal effect), and the channel-specific comparison (clearance-only sources) is authored-decoder only. The trained-decoder result compares against only 5 freshly-trained controls per arm (a coarse resolution) and is reported robust only when all three trainer seeds agree; the finer generic-vs-not-supported split cannot be decided by the predeclared C/M-max comparison alone (it would need a trained-null percentile floor, and this study's trained null is context-only), so it is reported as `no-specific-effect` — a reporting convention adopted after the trained scores were known, not itself a predeclared category — rather than forced into one of those two labels. `src/lib/ui/NullExplanationNote.svelte` renders one sentence stating the authored category (with the channel-specific modifier) and the trained result (robust or not, across the three trainer seeds), directly under the null-result explanation, with a link to `docs/pathway-interventions-report.md`. If the pinned `pathway-interventions-v1.json` artifact is missing (no manifest entry at all), the sentence is simply absent with no message; if it could not be fetched, it shows an honest "could not be loaded" message; if it fails its sha256 check, shape validation, or its cross-check against the shipped rewiring-null/null-explanation artifacts, it instead shows an honest "failed verification" message — none of these states affects the rest of the experiment or the note above it. |
| Lesion effect map | **Computed (offline)** | The activity view's optional "Lesion effect (offline)" color mode paints each of the 1008 neurons by the offline-computed paired change in `movementScore` when that single neuron is silenced for a whole episode (authored decoder, opponent parked, 100 held-out seeds), for whichever of the two atlas-covered topologies (biological, or the shipped rewired-seed-0 control) an arm's current topology matches — never a live/measured value, and never a claim about the real fly (see `docs/lesion-atlas-report.md` for the full method, statistics, and limitations, including the multiple-comparisons/FDR caveat). A diverging colormap centered at zero shows sign and magnitude; a neuron that did not survive per-graph Benjamini-Hochberg FDR correction (`q = 0.05`) is both color-faded toward neutral and marked with a non-color outline-ring, so significance is never encoded by color alone. An arm running disconnected has no atlas coverage and shows an explicit "no lesion data" state instead of a fabricated color. The atlas is loaded lazily (only once this mode is first selected) and sha256-verified, with `bodyIds` cross-checked element-wise against the loaded graph; a missing or failed-verification atlas disables the mode with an honest reason, and never affects the rest of the experiment or the "Displayed neural activity" (live rate) mode above. |

The application must not describe the POC as a brain emulation or imply that authored behavior is biological. `src/lib/ui/LedgerPanel.svelte` renders this table in-product next to the experiment's live results, plus links to the compiled artifact's manifest and ledger JSON (`public/data/malecns-arena-v1.manifest.json`/`.ledger.json` — exact dataset version, source-file hashes, compiler-revision hash, node/edge/contact counts, inclusion rules, and retained/dropped counts) and the CC BY 4.0 attribution for the source dataset (Janelia FlyEM Project (HHMI), MRC Laboratory of Molecular Biology, Google Research). See `docs/data-provenance.md` for the full sourcing detail those links point at.

The panel's "Readout (trained mode)" row is visible in both decoder modes (it documents what the trained-readout artifact *is*, not merely what is currently selected) and shows the artifact's sha256 prefix, its per-arm trainable-parameter count, its `D → H → 3` architecture, and links to `public/data/trained-readout-v1.report.json` and its manifest (base-path-safe static files) plus the human-readable `docs/trained-readout-report.md` on GitHub (`docs/` is not part of the deployed static site). If the artifact is missing, fails its sha256 check, or fails shape validation against the loaded graph, the row instead shows an honest "artifact failed verification" message with the reason, and the Trained decoder option is disabled with the same reason — Authored keeps working regardless.

### Topology labeling vs. the renderer's fixed arm identity

The 3D renderer (`src/lib/render/ArenaScene.ts`) gives the left agent a fixed "BIO" shape/label (a smooth icosahedron) and the right agent a fixed "REWIRED" shape/label (an angular octahedron with a wireframe overlay) — this is a stable *visual identifier for an arena position*, not a live claim about which topology that arm is currently running. `ExperimentPanel.svelte`'s topology selectors let either arm run biological, rewired, or disconnected; `TelemetryPanel.svelte` always shows each arm's actual selected topology as text next to its "(BIO shape)"/"(REWIRED shape)" label, so a user can never mistake a re-topologized arm for its default assignment.
